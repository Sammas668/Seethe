# Stage 4.7 Hotfix 4 — Marauder Sheet and Loadout Fidelity

This correction replaces the simplified Marauder template with the completed Tier I Reaver sheet supplied by the design workflow. It corrects the base attributes, saves, skills, proficiencies, Rage uses/duration/HP calculation, attack access, carrying thresholds and the complete standard loadout.

The character sheet now renders the live resolved values after Rage activates or ends and lists Armour, Belt, Backpack and Worn Utility separately.

This hotfix deliberately does not claim that every connected gameplay mechanic is complete. The release notes identify remaining mechanical gaps separately.
