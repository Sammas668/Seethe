# Stage 4.7 Hotfix 5 — Marauder Mechanics and Raider's Sack

## Scope

This hotfix connects the approved Tier I Marauder sheet to current tactical play while deliberately deferring systems not required by the farm raid.

## Rage and Fatigue

Rage is one use per mission, activates as a Quick Action and lasts seven rounds. It resolves the approved Strength, Constitution, HP, AC, Fortitude, Will, melee and Grapple values through the character resolver. Actions tagged as requiring calm, concentration or patience are rejected while Raging. Rage expiry applies the shared Fatigued effect: Strength −2, Dexterity −2, no Sprint and no second Rage for the encounter.

Rage and Fatigued use a secondary token-condition badge so they can coexist with Hidden or Alerted presentation. Critical life-state icons retain priority.

## Encumbrance

Current carried weight is calculated from existing item locations and body/captive ownership. The Marauder uses its approved 116/233/350 lb normal thresholds and 200/400/600 lb Raging thresholds. Light, Medium, Heavy and Over-Capacity states resolve to 80/60/40/0 feet of turn capacity; Sprint is 120/90/unavailable/unavailable. Mid-turn load changes never restore spent capacity.

## Raider's Sack

Every production Marauder receives one fixed Raider's Sack at Belt position (5,0), occupying the rightmost 2×2 cells of the 7×2 Belt. Clicking it opens a presentation-only 4×3 internal grid. The container accepts one restrained Medium-or-smaller captive or one item explicitly tagged as a compatible bulky burden. It rejects ordinary loot and a second entity.

The captive remains the same body-linked tactical item and character. Its weight includes body weight and carried equipment. Loading costs a Half Action; releasing to a legal adjacent tile costs a Minor Interaction. Extraction records the contained character as a captive rather than an item.

## Combat and equipment

Power Attack derives its legal setting from current BAB and the feat definition. Take Them Alive changes the existing rope/manacle restraint interaction to a Quick Action only when that Marauder caused the target's nonlethal Unconsciousness. Manacle stacks split one set at a time.

Grapple now has a minimum opposed-roll pipeline with Initiate, Release and Break Hold. The thrown Dagger preserves its item ID and lands as a ground item. Armour maximum Dexterity, armour-check penalty and weapon proficiency are resolved from equipment metadata.

## Deliberately deferred

Surrender, advanced restraint compatibility, Pin and assisted Grapple, traps, keys and locks, expanded rope use, burden noise, a new Bulk model, new Climb/Jump/Intimidate/Survival actions, level-3 Full Attack and five-foot adjustment remain outside this hotfix.
